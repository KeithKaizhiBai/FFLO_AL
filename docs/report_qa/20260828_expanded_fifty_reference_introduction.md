# Why the manuscript now uses a tiered 50-reference Introduction

The earlier 12-reference candidate was sufficient to prove citation
traceability, but it did not give readers enough context across four distinct
literatures: FFLO physics and candidate settings, altermagnetic
superconductivity, finite-momentum/topological superconductivity, and active
learning or scientific machine learning.

The revised policy separates citation quantity from evidentiary strength.
Twelve T1 sources remain the authorities for foundational or strong claims.
Thirty-eight T2 sources broaden contextual coverage after DOI and Crossref
metadata verification. T2 admission does not authorize a stronger scientific
claim, and no external reference is evidence for this project's numerical
Results.

The operational safeguards are:

- 45--55 admissible references, target 50;
- every bibliography entry must be cited in the Introduction;
- every cited key must belong to a scoped claim cluster;
- search-snippet-only sources are forbidden;
- uncited bibliography padding is forbidden;
- strong claims require primary or authoritative support;
- Stage III sensitivity and Stage IV nonclosure remain internal-evidence
  claims and retain their original caveats.

This gives the reader a broader map of the field without converting reference
count into a proxy for scientific support.
