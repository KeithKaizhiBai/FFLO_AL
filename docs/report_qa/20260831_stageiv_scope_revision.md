# Stage-IV threshold and manuscript-scope revision

## Question

Can the registered Stage-IV map-change, proxy-surface displacement, coverage,
trusted-surprise, and component-count cutoffs establish physical convergence
of the three-dimensional topology result?

## Answer

No. These quantities diagnose stability of a particular numerical sampling
and proxy-reconstruction procedure. Their cutoffs were not calibrated to a
thermodynamic free-energy scale, a bulk-gap scale, an invariant error bound, or
another physical resolution. Raw histories can document numerical evolution,
but cutoff crossings cannot by themselves establish physical closure.

## Manuscript consequence

- Remove Figure 4(d) and Stage-IV cutoff-based PASS/FAIL language from the main
  text.
- Retain Figure 4(a)--(c) as finite-domain exact observations.
- State that trusted topology support is discrete and that no continuous
  topology surface or complete three-dimensional atlas is inferred.
- Put raw histories in Supplemental Figure S1 without cutoff lines.
- Define the plotted quantities as numerical diagnostics and state that they
  are not thermodynamic observables or universal physical scales.

## Evidence retained

- 7,081 exact Stage-IV observations: 2,350 Normal, 141 uniform-SC, and 4,590
  FFLO.
- Trusted FFLO-only topology support: 3,145 cFFLO and 1,445 tFFLO.
- Four exact fixed-$\mu$ bins centered at $-0.45$, $0.15$, $0.85$, and $1.45$
  with half-width 0.025.
- Archived iteration histories and last-three raw diagnostic values, without
  interpreting operational cutoffs as physical scales.

This note is explanatory material only and does not alter the canonical model
or numerical definitions.
