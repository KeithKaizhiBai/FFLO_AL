# PRL-integrated main-text variant

## Question

How can the audited Active Learning--BdG manuscript be made to read like a
compact PRL-style paper without discarding the four-figure scientific story or
overwriting the existing reviewed manuscript?

## Answer

Use a separate publication profile rather than changing the core evidence
graph. The profile keeps a provisional Introduction, then interleaves each
result with only the methods needed to interpret it, and ends with Discussion.
Stage VI is not a standalone Outlook section; it is the final bounded outlook
paragraph of Discussion. The four registered Figure Engine/strict-data figures
appear close to their first substantive discussion and retain their frozen
captions in the first review candidate.

This restructuring does not change the model, numerical definitions, evidence,
claims, or physical labels. In particular, the Stage III $k=6$ sensitivity
deviation and the Stage IV finite-domain/nonclosure/no-continuous-surface
limitations remain visible. Stage V remains an exploratory negative result in
Discussion and does not establish learned-acquisition superiority.

The first integrated candidate is six pages and exceeds the internal planning
budget. That status is a transparent condensation signal, not a claim about an
official journal limit. External Introduction citations remain unresolved
because retrieval was not authorized. The candidate therefore stops at a
separate human-review gate and is neither scientifically nor publication
frozen.
