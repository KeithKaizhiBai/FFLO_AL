The workflow separates the expensive scientific oracle from the policy that
schedules it (Fig. 1). Thermodynamic classification follows the frozen
$F(\Delta,q)$ minimum, and topology is evaluated only for spectrally trusted
superconducting states. Reliability metadata therefore remains attached to
every accepted label and is available to both acquisition and stopping logic.
This hierarchy is the enabling component for scalable active learning: the
surrogate prioritizes exact BdG evaluations but never replaces the physical
solver.
