## Central scientific line

> Reliability-aware active learning enables exploration of thermodynamic,
> topology-resolved, and higher-dimensional FFLO phase spaces.

The paper combines a physics result with an enabling reliability framework:
expensive BdG labels retain authority, numerical risk is audited explicitly,
and active learning concentrates exact calls near thermodynamic and topology
structures in both low- and higher-dimensional parameter spaces.

## Approved section ownership by stage

- Stage I: main-text proof of concept for thermodynamic phase discovery.
- Stage II: main-text and Supplement exact-oracle reliability and numerical
  validation; present reliability as the enabling component for scalable
  active learning, including the 3D extension.
- Stage III: main-text topology-aware cFFLO/tFFLO discovery and convergence.
  Retain the configured $k=8$ pass, $k=6$ sensitivity deviation, and the fact
  that brackets are diagnostics only.
- Stage IV: main-text supported-with-caveat result and candidate Figure 4,
  demonstrating higher-dimensional exploration. State visibly that strict 3D
  topology convergence was not achieved.
- Stage V: Discussion only; exploratory learned-acquisition studies, with no
  superiority claim.
- Stage VI: Outlook only; future learned physical world models and EIG-based
  discovery, with no validated EIG claim.

audits, and the AI-assisted workflow. Discussion owns learned acquisition;
Outlook owns the ground-up world-model/EIG direction.

## Forbidden central claims

- V4 or any learned acquisition is superior to the production acquisition.
- The work provides a “complete 3D phase diagram” or “fully converged 3D
  topology atlas.”
- The world model is ready for EIG or EIG acquisition has been validated.
- Sparse Delaunay edges are topology boundaries.
- Absence of a finite-area nodal phase generalizes beyond the audited
  parameter/configuration scope.

This approval does not approve final rendered figures, external Introduction
retrieval, or the final manuscript.
