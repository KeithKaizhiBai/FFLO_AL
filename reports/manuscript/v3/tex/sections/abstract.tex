Resolving finite-momentum superconducting phases across expanding parameter spaces requires both reliable free-energy minimization and controlled use of expensive exact calculations. We retain an audited Bogoliubov--de Gennes (BdG) oracle as the sole source of thermodynamic and topological labels, while active learning only schedules the next exact evaluations. This separation yields an audited two-dimensional reconstruction of Normal, uniform-superconducting, and FFLO structure while keeping a hard-risk numerical layer explicit. On trusted gapped FFLO states, full-zone gap and Pfaffian diagnostics resolve connected cFFLO/tFFLO structure that passes the configured $k=8$ audit with a retained $k=6$ sensitivity. The same framework exposes thermodynamic and topology-aware structure in $(k_BT/t,J_A/t,\mu/t)$ across a finite sampled domain; the exact support is reported discretely, without inferring a continuous three-dimensional topology surface or a complete atlas.
