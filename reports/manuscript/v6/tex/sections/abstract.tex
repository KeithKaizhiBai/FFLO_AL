Finite-momentum superconducting phase diagrams require repeated minimization over pairing amplitude and momentum, followed by spectral tests wherever topology is meaningful.  We combine an exact Bogoliubov--de Gennes oracle with active learning that schedules, but never replaces, these calculations.  The method reconstructs the two-dimensional Normal, uniform-superconducting, and FFLO regions while evaluating 6,880 of 77,361 prescribed candidate locations, an exact-location sampling fraction of 8.89\% (11.24-fold reduction).  Restricting topology to reliable gapped FFLO states reveals connected conventional and topological FFLO support separated by a numerically reconstructed gap-closing region.  Extending the same procedure to $(k_BT/t,J_A/t,\mu/t)$ exposes systematic thermodynamic and topology-aware structure across a finite sampled domain.  The latter evidence is reported as discrete support and fixed-$\mu$ slices, not as a converged continuous topological surface or complete three-dimensional atlas.
