\paragraph{Probability conventions and exclusions.}
For the three phase probabilities, $P_{SC}=P_{\rm uniform}+P_{\rm FFLO}$, $H=-\sum_{a=1}^{3}P_a\log(\max(P_a,10^{-12}))/\log 3$, and $m=P_{(1)}-P_{(2)}$ with descending probabilities. These quantities are dimensionless. Member predictions are inverse-transformed before their population standard deviation is computed, so $\sigma_\Delta$ and $\sigma_q$ have the target amplitude and momentum units before the stated score normalizations.

The saved two-dimensional configurations declare a five-iteration recent-selection cooldown and a normal-side boundary-band mask, both using four-decimal coordinate keys. However, the recovered \texttt{discovery} branch explicitly clears both masks: they do not exclude candidates in the runs described here. Exact-coordinate exclusion and soft observation repulsion remain active. In the alternative refinement branch, recent selections come from iterations $\max(0,i-5),\ldots,i-1$; the boundary-band mask is collected from available merged records without this finite lookback. If the requested batch is underfilled, recent and then boundary-band exclusions are relaxed, while exact-coordinate exclusion remains. This distinction also applies to Stage III, whose saved mode is discovery. Its topology score replaces the score composition, while the interior penalty, eligible-pool quantiles, observation repulsion, and sequential batch repulsion remain inherited.

\paragraph{Stage-IV proposal construction and ordered channel selection.}
The proposal counts are computed by Python rounding of the normalized source fractions times 65,536, with any remainder assigned to global Sobol proposals. The block order is global Sobol, topology-bracket jitter, thermodynamic-bracket jitter, then chemical-potential edges. Opposite-label pairs are sought among eight normalized-coordinate neighbors, retaining only pairs with second index greater than the first and distance at most 0.20. Pairs are sorted by distance and truncated to the requested source count. The topology source uses reliable gapped superconducting support in the recovered adapter, not an implicit FFLO-only filter. Pair indices are sampled with replacement; midpoint jitter is independent Gaussian noise of standard deviation 0.018 times each domain span, followed by clipping.

For edge proposals, the temperature and exchange coordinates come from a Sobol draw. The first half of the chemical potentials are uniform within 0.04 of the domain span above its lower edge, and the remainder within that width below its upper edge. Missing bracket proposals are backfilled with a separate Sobol draw before eight-decimal proposal deduplication; no second fill is promised after deduplication. Proposal randomness uses seed $20260623+7717i$, with Sobol offsets $1000+i$, $2000+i$, and $3000+i$ for global, edge and backfill blocks, respectively.

Selection quotas use Python rounding of normalized channel weights times 256; the remainder is added to coverage. All phase-channel picks precede spectral picks, which precede coverage picks. Each pick updates the shared batch-repulsion product. A channel without finite positive weights falls back to the weighted combined score; invalid powered weights trigger the same fallback, and exhaustion terminates the batch. Selection uses seed $20260623+1000003i$. These details describe the stored selector and its configuration, not a newly executed reproduction of the selected sequence.
