\paragraph{Stored two-dimensional reconstruction algorithm.}
The topology audit uses inverse-square-distance averages of the saved $P_0$ and $P_\pi$ values over $k_{\rm nn}$ neighbors, with distances normalized by the parameter spans and a $10^{-9}$ distance floor. Predictions are supported only within distance 0.045 of a training point. The final audit mask additionally intersects this support with the convex hull of the final training coordinates. Here $k_{\rm nn}$ counts neighbors and is not crystal momentum $k$.

Zero contours of each reconstructed Pfaffian field are extracted only in the supported mask. Segments shorter than normalized arc length 0.0015 are excluded. A retained segment needs at least 75\% valid vertices and at least 10\% vertices supported either by both index classes within 0.045 or by a local bracket midpoint within 0.03. Brackets join distinct opposite-index neighbors separated by at most 0.03; duplicate unordered pairs are removed. They supply support diagnostics, not exact boundary locations or additional oracle calls. This describes the already stored reconstruction; no contour was re-extracted for this revision.
