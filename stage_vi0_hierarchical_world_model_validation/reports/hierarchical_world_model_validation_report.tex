The hierarchy resolves invalid-field mixing, but the full schema and uncertainty gates are not yet validated.\section{Scope}Frozen V3/V4 CSVs only. No exact solver, Delta-q search, active learning, HPC, or data edit. No learned state is transferable.\section{Hierarchy}Level 1 models SC existence and thermodynamics. Level 2 models the SC branch. Level 3 models trusted spectra. Level 4 models trusted gapped-SC Pfaffian outputs. Phase/topology labels are evaluation-only.\section{Matched smoke results}The mean spatial SC AUROC is 0.979. Median spatial $R^2$ is 0.441 for Level 1, 0.797 for Level 2, 0.051 for Level 3, and 0.912 for Level 4. Thermodynamics and $|q|$ are learnable. $P_0/P_\pi$ are learnable after trusted-gapped conditioning, but log-gap, $k_{gap}$, Pfaffian margin, and uncertainty-error alignment remain weak. Hierarchical Level-3 NLL improves over the same-split unified model; Level-4 improvement is not universal. Probe uncertainty is not monotonic across all run/level trajectories.\section{Critical answers}Thermodynamics can be learned independently. Conditioning makes $q$ learnable, but $\Delta F_{UF}$ cannot be tested. Topology regression becomes meaningful after invalid points are removed, yet its uncertainty is not consistently calibrated.
